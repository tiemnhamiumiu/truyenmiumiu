Mind Readers, Ruined Lives

When I was finally brought back to the Song family home, I shrank into myself, doing my best to look terrified and timid.

If I remembered correctly from what she said, my younger sister claimed our mother enjoyed beating her children. Would I get a beating the moment I set foot inside?

Sitting on the couch, our mother was comforting the fake heiress. Her hand froze mid-pat, her fingers going stiff against the girl's back.

My sister used to look at our father with such longing in her eyes. I wondered if she picked that habit up from our mother.

The woman’s face paled instantly upon hearing those words, her expression turning into a mask of pure shock and speechlessness.

My sister also mentioned that our older brother could only last for a single minute. What in the world did a minute even mean?

Standing nearby, my older brother’s expression darkened in an instant, his complexion turning a sickly, ghostly white.

I kept these thoughts rolling quietly through my mind, watching as the faces of everyone in the room shifted through a vibrant spectrum of colors.

All the while, I kept a subtle, innocent smile playing on my lips.

Chapter 1

On the day of my grand return, the fake daughter put on a display of utmost warmth and enthusiasm.

She rushed forward to take my hands, sobbing tears of soft sorrow. It is all my fault, she wept. I stole your place for so many years, forcing you to suffer endlessly. From now on, I want to be your little shadow. I will serve you in any way I can. As long as it makes you happy, I will do whatever you ask!

Seeing how reasonable and understanding Nora was, my biological mother felt a surge of deep affection and heartache. She pulled the girl into a tight embrace, soothing her softly.

Before anyone else was around, however, Nora had worn a completely different face.

Taking advantage of a moment when our parents were not looking, she had leaned close to my ear and whispered that I should never have come back.

She spun wild stories, claiming our mother was a cruel monster who delighted in abusing children, and that she herself had endured horrific beatings since childhood.

She fabricated tales about my childhood abandonment, insisting our father had deliberately discarded me out of sheer disgust.

She went so far as to frame our older brother as a twisted pervert who harbored depraved intentions toward his own sister.

I hope you manage to survive in this house, Nora had whispered, her face a picture of tragic worldliness.

I had feigned terror, though inside I was laughing at her desperate scheme.

Had I not already known the truth, her lies might actually have fooled me.

In the original timeline, Nora had systematically driven a wedge between my biological family and me.

Because they had raised her from infancy, our parents doted on her endlessly. That deep affection translated into cold neglect toward me, as they viewed me as an uncultured, uncouth girl raised in the destitute countryside.

My biological brother despised me just as deeply, constantly paranoid that my return would threaten his shared inheritance with the fake heiress.

Terrified by Nora’s relentless horror stories, the original owner of this body had pulled away from her relatives, which only deepened their disdain for her.

The nightmare reached its peak when Nora coerced a local vagrant into violating and murdering her. Not a single soul in the family stepped forward to seek justice for her death.

In the end, Nora seduced the older brother, worked her way into the father’s bed, seized the entirety of the Song family fortune, and lived out her days in glitz and glamour.

Now, however, I inhabited this body, fully aware of every twist and turn that was supposed to unfold. A spark of dark excitement stirred within me.

Nora said Mother loves to beat her kids. I wonder if I will get beaten up now that I am back.

After enduring endless beatings from my adoptive mother for so many years, I really do not want to be abused anymore.

Our mother’s hand, which had been gently soothing Nora’s back, suddenly halted. She stood rooted to the spot, staring blankly ahead.

Nora was the only one entirely oblivious to the sudden shift in the room, a triumphant smirk still playing at the corners of her lips.

They could all hear my inner thoughts. What none of them realized, however, was that everyone else in the room could hear them too.

Believing her scheme to alienate me had succeeded, Nora continued playing the sweet, obedient daughter. She stepped forward and wrapped her arm around mine.

Sister, Mother knew you were coming home today, so she personally prepared the best room in the house for you!

I followed her meekly toward the room.

Just as we crossed the threshold, my biological brother walked in from outside.

He immediately stepped in front of Nora, shielding her behind his back while tossing a icy warning in my direction. Everything in this house belongs to Nora. Do not think that just because you are back, you can take what is hers!

I shrank back, ducking my head and nodding frantically to show my terror.

Is this the older brother Nora said only lasts for a minute? What does lasting for a minute even mean? I am so curious to ask.

Then again, maybe I should not be curious at all. Nora said he is disgusting and constantly harbors sick thoughts about his sister. It sounds so terrifying!

The more terrified I pretended to be of the family, the wider Nora’s smile grew.

She stepped forward and clung to the brother’s arm, letting out a sweet, coaxing call, completely blind to the utter shock and horror painted across his pale face.

The moment she caught sight of him, Nora ran over out of habit.

She eagerly helped him loosen his tie and slip off his suit jacket, her body brushing lightly against his arm in a practiced, intimate gesture.

Wow, sister and Father are so close. But being this close seems a bit inappropriate, doesn't it? Especially since they are not actually blood-related father and daughter...

Both Nora and Father froze mid-movement.

The tips of Nora’s ears flushed red, and she whipped her head around to glare at me. Father coughed dryly twice, hastily taking a few steps back to put some distance between them.

Neither of them noticed that Mother, standing nearby, had developed a face as dark as the bottom of a charred pot.

He showed little enthusiasm for the return of his biological daughter, merely giving a vague nod before taking his seat at the dining table.

This was a daily ritual she used to parade her filial piety, earning endless praise from our parents about how sweet and considerate she was.

Nora lifted Father’s feet into the water, gently washing them while he leaned back on the lounger with his eyes closed in satisfaction.

Mother stood behind Nora, holding a fruit platter, though no one had noticed when she had walked up.

Just as Nora’s hands began to stroke upward toward Father’s thigh, my inner voice echoed clearly through the quiet room.

The way sister looks at Father is so passionate. I wonder if she learned that look from Mother. Though her charm falls way short of Mother’s.

Her bangs are a bit too thick and messy, though. If she trimmed them a little, her aura might actually surpass Mother’s.

One of Nora’s hands continued to stroke upward while the other instinctively reached up to adjust her bangs.

Nora! What on earth are you doing?

Mother’s furious shout broke the silence like a thunderclap.

Mother stormed forward, slamming the fruit platter onto the table with a sharp bang.

She glared daggers at Nora. What do you think you are doing? Where were your hands trying to go?

I... I... Nora stammered, completely lost for words.

Tears quickly welled in her eyes, falling in heavy, dramatic drops down her cheeks.

Father hurriedly spoke up. Why are you yelling at the child like that? You are scaring her. Doesn't she help wash our feet every single day?

Mother, Father worked hard all day and his legs were sore, so I was just trying to massage him a little, Nora sobbed out, sounding utterly wronged.

After all, Nora was a child Mother had raised from infancy, and a shred of maternal affection still lingered in her heart.

Seeing Nora weep, Mother’s heart softened slightly, her tone easing up just a bit. Still, the scene she had just witnessed planted a deep seed of suspicion in her mind. You do not need to wash our feet anymore. Spend your time studying instead. Your exams are right around the corner.

Nora bowed her head obediently, though her tightly clenched fists behind her back betrayed the fury raging within her.

Chapter 2

The Song family was undeniably wealthy and prestigious. Yet despite my official return, the room assigned to me was nothing more than a modest guest room.

As I was gathering my clothes to take a shower, Mother knocked and stepped inside.

I knew exactly why she had come. She wanted to help me bathe.

In the original timeline, this had been the sole opportunity for Mother and me to share a heart-to-heart conversation after my return.

However, the original girl had been brainwashed by Nora’s claims that Mother was a violent abuser, leading her to reject Mother's gesture out of sheer terror.

Mother had only offered out of polite courtesy back then, and after being rejected, she simply left. Over time, as the girl continued to avoid her, their bond withered into complete coldness.

I was no fool. When Mother asked if I needed help scrubbing my back, I immediately nodded in agreement.

Mother looked slightly taken aback before hastily retrieving a bath towel to assist me.

I did not hesitate to undress before her, revealing a back covered in a horrific tapestry of jagged scars.

Mother froze on the spot. Her hand trembled as she reached out toward my back, only to stop inches away, paralyzed by shock.

Harper... what in the world happened to you?

A forced, painful smile touched my lips as I gave her a small nod. It is okay, Mother. It is all in the past now.

Yet within my mind, a stream of quiet thoughts poured forth.

These scars were all left by my adoptive mother’s beatings, but I can never tell Mother that. I do not want her to worry.

When I was lost all those years ago, Mother must have suffered so much. If she knew how miserable my life was, she would only feel more heartbroken. I do not want her blaming herself.

I covertly studied Mother’s face and saw that her eyes were already rimmed with deep red.

Even if she had not built a strong bond with me yet, I was still her own flesh and blood.

Mother, what is wrong? I asked with innocent concern. These wounds do not hurt anymore. Please, feel free to touch them.

I am fine, Mother whispered, choking back a wave of emotion. Let me scrub your back.

She washed me with extreme care, her touch gentle as she went out of her way to avoid the deep and shallow scars covering my skin.

Mother is nothing like what sister described. She is not a cruel abuser at all. She is so gentle. Getting a bath from Mother makes me feel so happy.

Mother’s moving hand paused momentarily. Pretending not to notice, I allowed my internal thoughts to keep flowing.

Still, sister looks so strangely familiar to me. She looks like she was poured from the exact same mold as my adoptive mother.

This happens all the time in novels. When true and fake heiresses are swapped, it is usually planned by the fake heiress’s real parents. Could real life actually be this absurd?

Through the bathroom mirror, I caught a complex, sharp glint flashing across Mother’s eyes.

Once we finished, Mother walked out to the living room and called Father and my older brother, Ethan, over for a discussion.

Nora, naturally, was present as well.

Mother recounted the terrifying scars covering my body to everyone in the room. Father looked bewildered, while Ethan merely curled his lip in apparent indifference.

At that moment, Ethan and Nora stood close together, acting as intimate as they always did.

I knew that earlier, my dear brother had been unable to contain himself and had confronted Nora, demanding to know if she truly thought he was weak, pathetic, and disgusting.

Though startled that he had guessed her private thoughts, Nora had immediately denied everything to his face, making dramatic oaths to soothe him until their bond was as sweet and cozy as ever.

Mother spoke in a heavy tone. I have decided to report this to the police tomorrow. Harper’s adoptive mother is named Susan Miller, right? I am not letting that woman off the hook!

The moment the name Susan Miller left Mother’s mouth, Nora’s face transformed completely. Almost by reflex, she burst out in protest. You cannot go to the police!

Mother narrowed her eyes, her gaze fixing on her. Why on earth can we not go to the police?

Nora, of course, did not want the authorities involved. Susan Miller was her birth mother.

Years ago, Susan had worked as a midwife at the clinic where Mother gave birth. She had stolen me away, swapped our identities, and vanished without a trace.

Yet the two of them had reconnected long ago. Susan had set up a baked sweet potato cart right outside Nora’s school just to gaze at her biological daughter from afar every day.

Whenever Nora was in a foul mood, she would go to the cart to pick a fight, and every single time, Susan would shove me forward to serve as Nora’s personal punching bag.

According to Susan, Nora was a high-society lady from the wealthiest family in town, and my serving her was a privilege I should be grateful for.

Why doesn't sister want us to go to the police? Could my adoptive mother actually be her real mother? Good heavens, no wonder my adoptive mother always forced me to yield to her, constantly doting on her and sharing secrets with her! Have they known each other all along?

No!

Hearing my inner thoughts, Nora shrieked in panic. That is not true at all!

The moment the words left her mouth, she realized she had overreacted. She frantically clutched her clothes, trying to explain herself. She still raised you for all those years, Sister. If we make a huge fuss with the police, people will gossip and say the Song family is ungrateful. Father's business has been expanding lately, right? I am just worried it might hurt the family business.

Father nodded along in agreement upon hearing this. She makes a good point. There is no need to involve the police over this. Harper is back safe and sound now, so let the past be the past. Harper, what do you think?

He instantly dumped the burden of the decision onto my shoulders.

I felt nothing but cold contempt in my heart, but on the surface, I meekly lowered my head. I will do whatever Father thinks is best.

Father is so right. Even though my adoptive mother used to whip me with switch branches until I bled, made me sleep in the pigsty during winter, and forced me to eat whatever the pigs ate... even though she tried to sell me off to the old widower in the village to be a concubine, it is all in the past now. As long as I have enough food to eat and warm clothes to wear now, I am completely content.

Mother’s eyes burned red with suppressed rage, Father choked on his own breath, completely speechless, and Ethan stared in utter disbelief. Only Nora allowed a subtle, triumphant smirk to grace her lips.

It is getting late. Let me go make some warm milk for you to drink before bed, Mother, Nora offered sweetly.

Before she could finish her sentence, Mother let out an absolute roar. We are reporting this to the police!

Her tone was so unyielding that even Father knew better than to attempt to sway her.

Mother stared directly into Nora’s eyes, her voice dropping to a dangerous low. Nora, do you truly not know Susan Miller?

Nora’s entire body went rigid. I do not know her. I do not remember that woman at all.

Mother continued, Even though the family has brought your sister back, we never threw you out. You are still our daughter. I only hope you are not lying to me.

Mother, you raised me with your own hands. I would never lie to you, Nora sobbed, sounding deeply wronged.

The two men in the house could no longer sit still. They immediately stepped forward to defend her.

Mother, Nora is not that kind of person. Do not accuse her unfairly.

Ethan, she is the child we raised together. She would never lie to us.

A bitter, sarcastic smile curled the corner of my lips.

How tragic for the original girl. Even after hearing my inner thoughts exposed, this foolish father and brother still blindly took Nora’s side.

What they failed to realize was that every step they took to protect Nora only drove Mother’s face into a darker, more ominous shadow.

Chapter 3

When Mother recounted this to us, I was not surprised in the slightest.

Leading the charge was Chloe, Nora’s closest friend and the primary instigator behind the rumors spread against me in class.

Well, well, is this the little country bumpkin who just transferred to our class? I heard she is actually Nora’s older sister. Look at her, she does not have a shred of Nora’s elegance!

A sparrow trying to fly up a tree to become a phoenix! Does she think being taken in by the Song family makes her special? As far as we are concerned, Nora is the only true young lady of the Song family!

Not just anyone is qualified to be a daughter of the Song family. I suggest you pack your bags and head back to your pathetic little village!

A torrent of insults rained down on my ears, but a spark of dark excitement ignited in my eyes.

Just as expected, stepping into this school marked the beginning of a living nightmare for the original girl.

Because the Song family could not bear to throw Nora out, they had avoided publicly declaring the identity swap, merely announcing to the outside world that they had two daughters and that the older one had been lost in childhood.

In the end, she had collapsed into severe anxiety, falling deathly ill and dying in misery, a fate no less tragic than the years she spent under Susan’s thumb.

Is this person Chloe? I did not expect her to be so pretty. She looks nothing like the hideously ugly monster Nora described her as.

I heard Nora mention that she recently had a miscarriage. It seems to be true, given how ghastly pale her face looks.

Everything had veered completely off track from Nora’s plan.

If she lacked the courage, then I would gladly lend her a hand.

Is Mother here to pick us up? Sister once mentioned that she hated having Mother pick us up, saying she only liked it when Father and Brother came.

Mother’s entire body went rigid on the spot, and Nora froze in utter confusion.

Nora had never actually spoken those words to me, but deep down, that was precisely how she felt.

Mother looked at her with a icy expression, her voice filled with disgust. How old are you to be acting like this? Clinging and hugging right in front of the school gates, where is your self-respect? What will people think of our family’s upbringing if they see you acting like this?

With that, Mother turned toward me with a warm smile, waving her hand. Harper, come along. Let us head home.

I nodded obediently and followed Mother into the car.

The entire scene was captured by Chloe, who stood a short distance away, her eyes swirling with deep suspicion and intense calculation.

Even Nora usually knew better than to provoke her.

I saw a very handsome foreign guy near the school gates today with striking blue eyes.

I think he is Sophia’s boyfriend, though. But today I saw sister hand him something that looked like an aphrodisiac... She even made an appointment to meet up with him tonight.

One was Sophia, and the other was Chloe.

Seeing Nora beaten into such a pathetic state brought no pity to Mother’s heart.

How dare you bring your faces here to demand an accounting? Look at how you raised your daughter! She is barely a teenager and already learning how to seduce other girls' men. What a cheap little fox!

Even if it were true, she would never dare admit it in front of Father and Ethan.

Father and Brother are protecting sister so fiercely. They are so angry they have lost all sense of dignity, and now they want to drag Mother into this fight too. Do they only care about sister and not care about Mother at all?

Grace, you...

It seems that in the hearts of Father and Brother, Mother cannot hold a candle to sister.

Chapter 4

Human nature is inherently driven by jealousy, and Mother was no exception.

Seeing her husband and son join forces to protect an adopted child while turning their backs on her filled her with a toxic mix of betrayal and resentment.

The moment we stepped back inside the house, she declared in a cold, unwavering voice that she would no longer keep Nora under our roof!

No way!

Absolutely not!

Mother, Nora is my sister. She grew up in this house with us. How can you just throw her out like that?

Grace, aren't you being far too heartless? Nora is the child we raised together!

She went out of her way to seduce another girl's boyfriend! I will not have such a vile girl in my house!

With that, Mother reached out and pulled me into her arms. Harper is my one and only biological daughter!

That very evening, she casually knocked on Ethan’s bedroom door.

I used a hidden camera to record the entire encounter, a cold smirk hovering on my lips.
