The Songbird’s Runaway Secret.

After spending four years living as a gilded songbird by the side of Sean Vance, the most coveted heir in the capital, I made a startling discovery: I was pregnant.

A wave of panic surged through my heart. I tentatively breached the subject, asking whether he liked children.

The only response I received was a cold, indifferent stare and a ruthless answer.

"No. I've always hated them."

Yet, almost in the same breath, he made elaborate preparations to welcome his true love back into his estate, dedicated to personally taking care of her while she carried his twins.

My heart turned to cold ash, completely stripped of hope. I decided to stage my own death and silently flee the capital with my unborn child.

Unexpectedly, raising a child turned out to be astronomical in cost. To make ends meet, I had no choice but to become a young single mother sharing daily vlogs on social media. Unfortunately, during a minor mishap, I accidentally revealed my face.

That very night, the apartment complex where I lived was surrounded by dozens of men in dark suits, lined up so tightly that not even an ant could slip through.

Sean stepped out from the shadows, blocking my only way out. His voice was laced with a terrifyingly suppressed rage.

"Chloe, how dare you go online and claim to be a widow whose husband passed away?"

My young daughter, who had never seen her father before, peeked her tiny head out from behind the front door, asking curiously in a sweet voice:

"Mister, my daddy isn't dead yet. He's just an irresponsible scumbag!"

I stood there, utterly speechless.

Please, my sweet little princess, shut your mouth right now!

-----

Chapter 1

I, Chloe, used to be an exceptionally dedicated songbird.

Four years ago, Sean Vance, the famous heir who ruled the capital's high society, rescued me on a stormy night while inspecting a small coastal town. At the time, I was on the verge of death.

That fateful rescue did not just pull me out of the mud; it sent me straight into his bed.

Everyone in high society whispered that Sean Vance was a cold, unfeeling man. No woman had ever stayed by his side for more than two dates.

Yet he kept me around, keeping me in a luxurious grand estate for four whole years. That added up to over a thousand nights of intimacy.

Thanks to the blessings of this capital elite, I naturally stepped into the world of upper-class luxury, frequently accompanying wealthy socialites and heiresses as we browsed high-end silk and fashion boutiques.

While other women shopped to enhance their own beauty, I relied on my professional work ethic as a kept woman, carefully choosing nightwear based entirely on the tastes of my benefactor.

I had just swiped my card to purchase a sheer silk negligee when my phone buzzed with a text message.

"That's too cheap. The material definitely isn't good enough. Pick another one."

Did this man seriously have to supervise even this? Talk about overly demanding!

Still, remembering that he was the generous patron who funded my entire lifestyle, I grit my teeth and endured it.

Just as I was enthusiastically selecting a second item, my gaze froze on a striking advertisement nearby featuring a gorgeous woman.

Underneath the large portrait were the words:

Brand Ambassador: Michelle Lu.

The moment I silently repeated the name in my mind, a strange, robotic voice chimed inside my head.

It announced that I had activated a key trigger word, revealing a earth-shattering secret: I was merely the villainous female supporting character in a romance web novel titled "The Billionaire's Precious Babies."

Because the shock was so overwhelming, I couldn't remember any other plot details, except for one crucial fact. As the evil side character, I was destined to give birth to a daughter later in the story.

How absurd!

Every single time Sean was intimate with me, he used strict protection. How could I possibly get pregnant with his child?

Was this some kind of new scam sweeping the country?

And yet, when I finished my shopping trip and returned to the estate, I caught the butler's little granddaughter playing with a sewing kit in the master bedroom.

The little girl's hands were trembling as she used a sharp needle to thread a long string of protective rubber items into a garland.

It turned out that all those supplies had been poked full of holes in a way no one could have ever anticipated.

Was the universe seriously playing a cruel joke on me?

I remained lost in deep thought for a long time, my mind completely scrambled until late into the night.

I was so out of it that I didn't even notice when Sean returned home.

He had just showered, his lean and muscular frame leaning softly over from the other side of the king-sized bed.

Warm vapor and a gentle kiss falling on the back of my neck pulled me back to reality.

"What's on your mind?"

Sean's voice was slightly raspy, carrying a lazy undertone.

This was his signature tone whenever his emotions were stirring. He was used to taking control, wanting me to yield to him first during every conversation.

Yet tonight, no matter how skilled he was, I couldn't focus at all.

Sean was clearly displeased. His large hands tightened around my waist, lifting me effortlessly onto his solid chest.

A ticklish sensation rushed over me, forcing me to open my hazy eyes.

"Sean..."

At first, Sean knitted his brows, showing his annoyance. But seeing my pitiful expression, his voice softened into a gentle coaxing.

"Be good. Is something wrong? If you're unhappy, just tell me."

Remembering the strange mechanical voice from earlier that afternoon, I couldn't help but ask out loud:

"It's just... do you have any intention of getting married in the future?"

According to the plot guidelines, I was the villainous supporting character who desperately craved to marry the male lead, Sean Vance.

In the early days, even though Sean spent money recklessly to keep me, he only viewed me as an outlet for his desires.

The person truly engraved in his heart was the female lead, Michelle Lu, who was currently struggling in the overseas entertainment industry.

Once Michelle returned, all his attention would shift entirely to her, and I would be cast aside completely.

These events were predetermined.

For instance, the moment I dared to ask for a title or commitment, he would instantly turn cold.

Sure enough, as soon as the question left my lips, Sean's voice dropped, turning icy without a trace of warmth.

"Never mention marriage to me again."

"I don't want to hear those two words ever again."

Every single word matched the script perfectly.

It seemed a supporting character like me didn't need to waste energy planning a way to run away after all. I was going to be thrown out anyway.

I instantly lost all spirit, unsure whether I was disappointed by his blunt answer or secretly celebrating the fact that I had seen through the truth early.

Still, what if I was just a supporting character? I had been used to being an extra existence since childhood anyway.

That night, after satisfying his needs, Sean left the bed and did not return to the master bedroom.

The next morning, I heard from the head housekeeper that he had stayed in the study all night, smoking one cigarette after another.

For the next half month, he vanished completely, never setting foot back in the estate.

Chapter 2

I found myself with a rare streak of free time to scroll through social media.

While trying to decide where to settle down after leaving this place, an image of a peaceful coastal island filled with spring colors caught my eye, causing me to make a choice on impulse.

On second thought, as an evil supporting character destined to be dumped, the most urgent task right now was to gather more savings.

So I left a dissatisfied comment on the post:

"The price is way too expensive. I don't have enough money to buy it!"

To my absolute shock, just an hour later, my bank account received a transfer of one million dollars from Sean Vance.

Was this guy serious? After declaring he would never marry me and ghosting me for half a month, did he really think throwing money around could solve everything?

Immediately after, transfer notifications started flooding my phone in rapid succession: one million, another million, and then another...

The total reached a smooth nineteen point ninety-nine million dollars.

Well, okay. Spending money was indeed very effective.

I bounced up from the velvet armchair, put on refined makeup, and dressed up before stepping out the door.

Before heading to his office, I made sure to stop by a roadside diner to buy a container of chicken soup that looked like I had spent hours simmering it myself.

---

When I reached the executive floor of his corporate headquarters, I happened to overhear a conversation inside his half-open office door.

"Michelle is coming back soon. Did you know?"

"Yeah."

It was Sean's familiar, deep, and distant voice.

"She must have dropped hints to you already. But rumor has it she caught wind that you've been keeping a little songbird on the side..."

Before the other man could finish his sentence, Sean cut him off sharply.

"It's fine. I have my own ways of handling her."

My heart shook violently.

Handling me?

I was about to press my ear closer to listen further when I was suddenly interrupted by a secretary behind me.

The office door opened, and the man who had been chatting inside stepped out.

He was clearly Sean's close friend from his teenage years, a face I had seen in finance magazines before.

However, Sean had never introduced any of his friends to me, so I didn't know his exact identity.

Thinking about it now, it was probably because I was just a meaningless supporting character not worth introducing to his inner circle.

Curious, I turned back to glance at the man a few more times.

The moment I turned around, though, I bumped straight into Sean's icy face.

Even though I was already planning my smooth departure to sever all ties, I still forced a sweet smile to greet him.

"Mr. Vance, have you been really busy lately? Are you feeling tired?"

Sean didn't say a word. He reached out his left hand and decisively locked the office door behind us.

He turned, lifted me up, and set me down right onto his massive executive desk.

After four years of living together, Sean knew my temperament inside and out.

"The second you step out the door, your eyes are glued to another man? You don't even bother looking at your own man?"

"While I was away from home, you didn't send a single text or make a single call. It seems you didn't miss me at all."

"Chloe, are you looking for trouble again?"

My mind went completely blank, incapable of registering anything he said. It felt as though fireworks were exploding in front of my eyes.

In the chaotic intensity, I couldn't help but softy curse under my breath:

"Sean Vance, you jerk! Ah!"

"Do all your subordinates and female admirers know you like playing with bunny ears in bed like this?!"

"Your stamina is terrible! Are you getting old and losing your touch?"

The consequence of bursting out with insults turned out to be far worse.

Afraid that the loud noise would be heard by people outside, I quickly realized my mistake and pleaded for mercy.

"Fine, fine! You're the best, okay?"

"Sean, you're not old. You're the boss! You're my daddy!"

"Daddy, please!"

The moment those words left my mouth, the veins on Sean's neck bulged with intense excitement.

He lowered his head and delivered a deep, overwhelming kiss.

Dammit! Why on earth did that title turn him on so much?

However, the person who received the ultimate shock shortly after was me.

I was pregnant.

That afternoon, when I walked out of his office with trembling legs, I felt a dull ache in my lower abdomen.

The pregnancy test showed two bright, glaring red lines.

It turned out the pregnancy had occurred earlier. The plot progression truly was an force that couldn't be resisted.

The mechanical voice chimed in my head once more, informing me that the baby in my tummy would be a girl.

In the future, driven by wild jealousy, I would swap my daughter with Michelle Lu's daughter, and then abuse the child.

After a series of baby-swapping tragedies, the truth would eventually be exposed. Both children would end up loving Michelle Lu.

Sean would also hate me to the core, so much that he wouldn't even want to look at my face again.

In the end, I would be thrown into an underground auction house, enduring endless humiliation and torment, living a life worse than death.

My face turned pale. I had to sit down and rest for a long time before regaining my composure.

I made up my mind on the spot: I had to leave this place immediately.

This child was mine. I refused to follow in my own mother's footsteps, harboring resentment toward the father and heartlessly abandoning my own flesh and blood.

The savings I had accumulated were more than enough to raise this child to adulthood.

I packed a few simple belongings and silently slipped away from the estate in the middle of the night.

Ironically, my first escape attempt didn't go smoothly at all.

Originally, I planned to land in a small city near the capital before transferring to another flight.

Unexpectedly, the moment I stepped out of the ticket gate, someone was already waiting for me. It was none other than Sean's head assistant.

"Miss Chloe, Mr. Vance mentioned that since you left in such a hurry, you must have left many belongings behind. He sent me here to shop for whatever you need."

The assistant spoke respectfully as he handed me a phone with an active call.

I took it blankly like a robot.

From the other end of the line, Sean's deep, slightly hoarse voice echoed through the speaker.

"Why did you leave in such a rush? Did you want to go somewhere for a vacation?"

"Yeah..." I tried my best to keep my voice as natural as possible. "It was boring staying at home, and you refused to come back. Where are you right now?"

"I'm overseas handling some business."

I knew perfectly well he was over there to pick up Michelle Lu and her pregnant self.

Still, I deliberately asked:

"Is it something important?"

"Just business."

I fell silent for a few seconds. My eyes unconsciously drifted down to my still-flat belly, and I suddenly spoke up:

"Sean, do you like kids?"

Silence stretched across the line for a moment.

Then came a few words laced with clear distaste.

"I don't like kids. In fact, I detest them."

I froze.

Then, a bitter smile crept onto my lips.

"What a coincidence. So do I."

I didn't tell Sean that I was running away.

Having read plenty of web novels over the years, I understood that Sean would definitely send people to secretly follow and monitor me due to his possessive nature.

I also understood that since this relationship began with his word, it had to end with a decision from his side too.

However, I didn't want to be like the heroines in books who had to go through ninety-nine ridiculous escape attempts.

Not only because I lacked the power to fight against a powerful heir like Sean Vance, but also because before long, my baby bump wouldn't be something I could hide from anyone.

Under the assistant's watchful "protection," I spent a few days sightseeing before following him back to the familiar estate.

Sean remained absent, never showing his face once.

According to circulating entertainment news, Michelle Lu had landed in the capital yesterday.

Not a single photo was leaked. Security had been tightened to the absolute maximum, something only top-tier elite families could arrange so seamlessly.

Late at night, after waiting for the servants to retire, I pulled out my phone and dialed a familiar number.

"I need a death certificate and a completely new identity."

Chapter 3

The person I called was none other than Julian Vance, Sean's childhood friend.

He was also the exact man I had crossed paths with outside Sean's office that day.

The reason I reached out to him was that after that brief encounter, Julian had secretly arranged a private meeting with me.

He had declared that he viewed Michelle Lu as his own sister and would never stand by and watch her get hurt.

He added that Sean and Michelle were a match made in heaven, advising me to know my place and step down gracefully.

Oh, know my place? I was an absolute expert at that. Otherwise, how could I have lasted four years as the kept woman alongside the most difficult heir in the capital?

Even so, Julian couldn't hide his shock when he received my call.

After all, forging a death certificate and establishing a new identity was no easy task, even for someone with his resources.

"I'm begging you, it's just breaking up with Sean. Does it really need to be this complicated?"

I recounted my recent bitter failure at escaping.

After listening, Julian couldn't help but swear.

"Dammit, Sean actually put tailing details on you?"

I immediately replied with sarcasm:

"Well, yeah. Who told me to be so irresistible?"

Julian scoffed. "You little temptress, you really have no shame, do you?"

"Julian," I warned coldly, "try insulting me one more time. Believe it or not, I'll stop trying to leave, stay right here, and ruin things between your precious Michelle and Sean forever."

In the end, Julian had no choice but to tone down his voice and apologize.

He grit his teeth and spoke through his tight jaw:

"I can arrange the death certificate and the new identity for you. But with Sean keeping you under soft house arrest, how are you planning to break free?"

"A woman living off a man... tsk, I don't believe you have the skill to outsmart Sean."

As it turned out, he was completely wrong.

I wasn't just a simple woman living off a man.

I was someone who had devoured hundreds of romance novels and mastered the Art of War!

Taking a deep lesson from my previous failure, I acted with extreme caution for my second attempt.

Ever since my sudden solo trip, Sean had undoubtedly grown suspicious. No matter where I went, bodyguards were discreetly trailing me from a distance.

Even during casual shopping trips, black sedans were constantly idling near the mall exits.

I feigned perfect obedience for two solid weeks. On a quiet afternoon, I took the initiative to call Sean, inviting him to have dinner together.

More than a month had passed, and Sean had not set foot in the estate once.

His calls and texts had gradually grown sparse. Occasionally, he would send a brief message out of routine, asking if I was behaving myself.

The tone was clearly just going through the motions.

After all, his destined female lead had returned. It was time for a villainous supporting character like me to exit the stage.

Yet, to my surprise, Sean actually agreed to the invitation.

"I might be a bit late tonight."

"If you're hungry, go ahead and eat first."

Although I saw through everything in my heart, I still acted spoiled on the outside.

"Mr. Vance, that's so mean. How could you leave me to eat all by myself?"

"Be good. Listen to me, alright?"

This was the first time Sean coaxed me when we weren't in bed.

That deep, lingering voice made my mind sway for a brief second.

"I'll definitely make it tonight. I have something I want to tell you in person. Wait for me, okay?"

"Okay."

I obeyed softly and sweetly, causing his guard to drop completely.

That afternoon, I went shopping as usual. Inside the shopping center, I spotted a girl with a figure very similar to mine. I paid her to put on my outfit and take a cab to the airport.

Sure enough, the moment that girl appeared at the airport, she was immediately intercepted by Sean's men.

Taking advantage of the chaos, I disguised myself as a mall employee and smoothly snuck out through the service exit.

The moment I escaped the surveillance and stepped into the private car waiting for me, my phone rang frantically in my pocket.

It was Sean calling.

Looking at the childish caller ID on the screen: "Miss Chloe Loves Mr. Vance the Most."

In an instant, countless old memories flooded my mind.

I had set this contact name during a business trip to the far north with Sean to watch the aurora.

Under the brilliant night sky, I had taken the initiative to kiss him for the first time, then secretly changed his contact name on my phone.

It wasn't that we never had sweet moments.

Even if that sweetness was perhaps entirely one-sided on my part.

Watching the phone screen flash continuously, I gently placed my hand over my still-peaceful belly. The stinging sensation in my eyes gradually faded, replaced by a tranquil smile.

"Mr. Vance, thank you for giving me your care and warmth. I don't regret knowing you."

"Goodbye... never see you again!"

---

Eight months later, I gave birth to my daughter, Penny, in a peaceful coastal city.

From then on, my life gained a precious presence that I could never let go of. It became busy, yet filled with profound happiness.

After my daughter was born, I didn't go out looking for a regular job.

First, taking care of a newborn by myself made it extremely difficult to manage time.

Second, the day I left the capital, I had accumulated a substantial amount of money.

However, when Penny turned four, I realized I couldn't just sit around wasting my savings forever.

My daughter was sweet and easy to care for; eating a simple small pastry was enough to keep her happy all day. Yet I always wanted to give her the best things in life.

Deep down, I couldn't erase the guilt I felt toward her. Because of my selfish choice, she had been deprived of a father's love since the day she was born.

After much deliberation, I decided to create an account on social media and become a small content creator.

Initially, I just wanted to give it a try. Unexpectedly, Penny was so adorable and witty that she quickly captured people's hearts, becoming a popular child sensation.

Every video or picture of her generated an astonishing amount of engagement.

Before long, I accumulated over a million followers and even started hosting live streams.

Out of caution, I never showed my full face on camera, always wearing a mask to cover up.

This only made the netizens even more curious.

Some viewers even left teasing comments, asking if I was a runaway bride being desperately hunted down by some wealthy elite family.

I never responded, nor did I take it to heart.

Until one day during a live broadcast, Penny was playing beside me and accidentally knocked off my mask.

The live chat exploded instantly!

Countless messages flooded the screen in a blur, almost all of them praising my appearance.

I hurriedly put the mask back on, thinking that showing my face for just a few seconds wouldn't cause any real harm.

However, a sudden comment caught my eye, leaving me frozen on the spot.

"Hey, am I the only one who noticed? Why does Penny's mom look exactly like the woman on Sean Vance's phone wallpaper?"

Chapter 4

Panicked, I abruptly shut down the live stream.

After coaxing my daughter to sleep, I hid in the home office and turned on my laptop.

This was the first time in five years that I actively searched for news about Sean Vance.

Sean had always hated appearing in the media, so information about him was extremely scarce.

Thanks to the sleuthing skills I had developed over the years, I managed to find a photo taken at a business gala posted by a small account.

The photo wasn't very clear. A tall, handsome man stood in a dark corner, his head slightly lowered as he stared at his phone screen.

The dim lighting cast soft shadows over his sharp features, vaguely revealing intense emotions in his eyes.

On his phone screen was a slightly blurry photo of a woman.

The girl was smiling sweetly, holding a bright red cherry between her slender white fingers.

Below the photo, many people were still guessing the identity of this mysterious woman.

Quite a few fans of Michelle Lu left wild comments, claiming it was definitely their idol, and even spreading rumors that she and Sean had a child together, speculating it was a secret high-society marriage.

Yet I sat there, completely dazed.

Because the person in that photo... was me.

It was me when I was twenty-one, right after I had become the songbird by Sean's side.

But why would Sean use my photo as his phone wallpaper?

I couldn't figure it out no matter how much I thought about it.

In the end, I brushed it aside and stopped overthinking.

That had been my philosophy of survival ever since the day I left him.

Five years had passed. Sean and I had long become familiar strangers.

I reminded myself that if he didn't love me back when I gave him my whole heart, then whatever he did now had nothing to do with me anymore.

I quickly posted a short statement on my account:

"I am a single mother raising Penny on my own. Her father passed away a long time ago..."

Fortunately, society was much more open-minded now. Once the statement was posted, fans left plenty of supportive and encouraging messages.

I thought this matter would quietly blow over.

Yet that very night, as I carried groceries back from the convenience store with Penny, my instincts picked up on something unusual in the apartment complex.

The elderly residents who usually sat outside playing chess and enjoying the evening breeze had vanished without a trace.

Even the doorman and security guards were nowhere to be found.

The entire place was deathly quiet, devoid of a single soul.

Penny stepped closer, tugging at my skirt with her tiny hand, whispering fearfully:

"Mommy, why is it so quiet? I'm scared."

Panic surged in my chest as well. I bent down, scooped Penny up into my arms, and rushed up the stairs.

The moment I stepped into our apartment, my eyes dropped to the floor.

The tip of my shoe, which I had intentionally set at an angle before leaving, had been shifted slightly.

Someone had broken into the apartment!

Real panic set in.

I recalled the strange mechanical voice that had chimed in my head five years ago.

Then I thought about the face-revealing incident on the live stream today.

This was a novel world.

Could Michelle Lu have discovered Penny's existence... and sent people to capture us?

Not daring to gamble with my daughter's safety, I quickly wrapped Penny in a thick coat, held her tight, and sprinted toward the back exit of the complex.

However, the back door was bolted shut.

What was even more terrifying was that even the small gap at the bottom of the wall had been filled in!

It had been sealed shut with solid cement!

I stood there as if struck by lightning.

Good grief...

Right at that moment, rapid, synchronized footsteps echoed from behind, sounding disciplined and strictly trained.

A line of bodyguards dressed in black suits approached, forming two neat rows.

Sean Vance, wearing a dark gray trench coat, emerged coldly at the end of the line.

The moment my eyes met Sean's icy gaze, only two words flashed through my mind.

I'm doomed!

Penny was over four years old now. She ate well and had grown quite sturdy, so I rarely carried her for long periods anymore.

But right now, I hugged her tightly against my chest, not daring to loosen my grip even a little.

The little girl in my arms whimpered softly:

"Mommy... Mommy, you're squeezing my arm. It hurts..."

I choked on my words, unconsciously stepping backward.

Bit the only retreat path had already been blocked.

When my back hit the cold wall, Sean had already stepped right up to me.

His tall frame, which used to bring me a sense of security, now made me tremble with fear.

I swallowed hard, trying to act calm.

"Hello, Mr. Vance..."

"Four years, eight months, and seventeen days."

Sean's deep, chilling voice sounded.

Before I could process what he meant, he let out a cold, mocking laugh.

"Chloe, you're really good at running away."

"Go ahead. Try running now. Let's see you run!"

I stood there, completely silent.

Penny squirmed in my arms and raised her head.

"Mommy... Mommy, I can't breathe!"

Strangely enough, my daughter was usually afraid of even small cats, yet facing Sean, who was radiating an intimidating aura, she showed no fear at all.

She quickly wriggled against my side, slipped out of my arms, and trotted right over to Sean, staring up at him with wide eyes.

"Handsome mister, you're so tall and handsome!"

"Do you have a wife yet?"

Oh, heavenly spirit!

Why on earth did I give birth to such a silly little girl?

I frantically tried to figure out a way to cover Penny's mouth.

But the moment I made a move, Sean, thinking I was trying to bolt, took a step forward and used his large hand to pull me firmly toward him.

His other arm wrapped tightly around my waist.

His entire body pressed flush against mine.

The intimate contact after nearly five years apart sent an electric shock straight through my system. I hurriedly reached out to push him away.

As a result, after a few shoves, I felt something...

My cheeks instantly flushed bright red!

"It's a pity I didn't bring a weapon, otherwise I'd love to send you straight to the afterlife with one shot."

Sean grit his teeth, whispering harshly in my ear:

"However, a man's natural weapon was made specifically for you. Can you feel it?"

"Just this alone is enough to deal with you."

"Chloe, you owe me nearly five years. How many times is that enough for me to make you pay back, huh?"

I could barely keep my footing.

"You!"

You absolute rogue!

I was completely rendered speechless by Sean's raw, unfiltered words.

Naturally, I didn't dare to use force to push him away anymore.

Just like that, I remained stiff as he carried me upstairs in his arms.

Penny trotted along behind us, her face filled with intense curiosity.

Perhaps because the large group made quite a bit of noise, when we arrived at our front door, Mrs. Wagner, our next-door neighbor, opened her door and peeked out.

"Hey, Chloe, who is that man next to you?"

Sean held me tightly, answering with absolute composure:

"I am her child's father."

Mrs. Wagner's eyes widened in surprise.

"Penny's father? No way, Chloe, didn't you tell me last time that you were a widow?"

I squeezed my eyes shut in embarrassment.

The front door slammed shut behind us.

Sean's face shifted from icy cold to a dark, furious rage.

"Chloe, you actually dared to go online and declare that you're a widow whose husband passed away?"

Penny ran over to pour a glass of water. Hearing this, she poked her small head out from behind the bedroom door.

"That's not true, handsome mister! My daddy isn't dead. He's just an irresponsible scumbag."

"A scumbag who doesn't know how to cherish my mommy! Mister, you must never learn from a guy like that!"

I buried my face in my hands.

Please, my sweet little princess, shut your mouth right now!

Chapter 5

Sean sat down on the sofa in the living room.

Without that overwhelming, intimate pressure pinning me down, my courage gradually returned.

I composed myself and spoke up:

"Mr. Vance, it's getting late..."

Sean cut me off coolly:

"It certainly isn't early anymore. You've been hiding for four years, eight months, and seventeen days."

I fell quiet.

His gaze shifted over to Penny.

His tone unconsciously softened considerably.

"Come here to me, sweetie."

Before I could stop her, my silly daughter had already trotted over, obediently climbing right onto Sean's lap.

This was the first time I had ever seen Sean holding a child. The image was completely out of sync with his usual noble, distant aura as a high-society heir.

As Sean gently stroked Penny's hair, he suddenly asked me:

"She doesn't know who her father is?"

I immediately countered:

"Mr. Vance, you've misunderstood. Penny is three and a half years old this year. Her father passed away suddenly two years ago..."

Because I had assumed a new identity, Penny's age had been adjusted accordingly to match the records.

Sean didn't even bother looking at me. He used his long finger to lightly pinch Penny's chubby cheek.

"Did you hear that? Your mom says you're only three and a half, but the cake I mentioned earlier is only for kids who are four and a half. I guess I can't buy it for you then."

Hearing that, Penny immediately shook her head like a rattle.

"That's wrong! Mommy made a mistake! I'm four years and eight months old! I can eat the cake!"

Sean narrowed his eyes, casting a deep, intense look in my direction.

I stood there, totally exposed.

Only then did I realize where my daughter got her sharpness from. Sometimes I even felt embarrassed that I couldn't keep up with her quick mind.

It turned out it was all genetics!

The icy expression Sean had maintained all evening finally relaxed slightly.

His voice was gentle, carrying a somewhat awkward sweetness as he coaxed Penny:

"Would you like to have a daddy?"

"I want..."

Just as Penny started to answer, Sean's phone sitting on the table suddenly rang.

Penny's restless little hand accidentally swiped across the screen, connecting the call.

From the speaker came the loud, crying voice of another little girl:

"Sob... Daddy..."

I froze completely.

Penny also sat there dazed.

Sean quickly disconnected the call.

However, Penny had already realized what was happening. Her small lips pressed into a tight line.

"Handsome mister, you lied to me. You already have a daughter! Penny doesn't need you anymore..."

Rubbing her reddened eyes, the little girl sniffled and ran straight into the bedroom.

Seeing my daughter cry made my heart ache terribly. I immediately chased after her and locked the bedroom door tight.

I held her in my arms to comfort her, while Sean kept knocking on the door from outside.

"Chloe, be good and open up."

"Penny, open the door. Let daddy explain."

"That call just now wasn't what it..."

"I don't want to hear it!"

I sharply interrupted him, a bitter wave of grievance swelling up and filling my chest.

"Mr. Vance, I admit that four years ago I deceived you and ran away. I did spend a lot of your money..."

"However, you already have Michelle Lu, and the two of you have a child together. Please stop bothering my daughter and me! Don't hurt my child anymore!"

"During those four years, I was your kept songbird, but I, Chloe, will absolutely never accept being a third party!"

Sean's voice outside the door suddenly dropped to an absolute low.

"How do you know about Michelle Lu? What did she say to you?"

I choked back a sob:

"I know you love her! I know everything!"

"Chloe, it's not like that..."

"Then what is it? Go ahead, explain it clearly if you're so capable!"

I threw caution to the wind, shouting my defiance at the man outside.

"The person I love is..."

Before Sean could finish his sentence, I faintly heard a sharp, painful gasp from outside.

Then came a heavy, dull thud, sounding as though someone had collapsed onto the floor.

After comforting Penny until she stopped crying, I cautiously opened the door and stepped out. However, the living room was completely empty.

It was as if Sean Vance had never set foot here at all.

---

After that night, Sean didn't show up again.

I secretly thought that perhaps he had thought things through. To him, I was merely a possession he wanted to control, someone he couldn't bear to let go of simply because he hadn't found me.

He had probably returned to Michelle Lu and their child.

I resumed my quiet, peaceful routine with my daughter.

Half a month later was Penny's birthday. She invited a few friends from daycare over to celebrate.

Julian Vance also traveled all the way from the capital.

More than four years ago, he had helped me escape from Sean. Afterward, I wanted to send him money to express my gratitude, but he adamantly refused.

When Penny was born, I was all alone in a strange city, and Julian had supported me a great deal.

The man had a sharp tongue, mockingly calling me a temptress every time we met.

On the surface, I snapped back at him, but deep down, I understood.

He was Sean's close friend and viewed Michelle as a sister. Naturally, he couldn't feel fondness toward a nameless songbird like me.

Even though he helped me out of a desire to clear the path for Michelle, I remained grateful for his assistance.

For Penny's birthday this time, he brought along extremely expensive gifts.

While the children were playing inside, I took the opportunity to take out the trash downstairs, and Julian followed behind.

"Chloe."

Julian suddenly called out from behind.

I turned my head. "Yeah?"

"Can I call you Chloe from now on?"

Julian smiled faintly as he spoke:

"No more calling you a temptress."

I curled my lips. "Forget it, I'm used to hearing it anyway."

I tried to walk past him to go back inside.

However, Julian reached out and grabbed my wrist tightly.

"Chloe."

I suddenly realized his attitude felt somewhat off.

I heard his voice drop low:

"Penny is growing up. I hear the kids in her class always asking about her father. Have you ever considered finding a father for her?"

"I used to have prejudices against you, but later I realized that you and Sean aren't what I thought."

"I know you truly liked him, which was why you were willing to yield and stay by his side."

"I also know you're actually a kind and optimistic girl. Chloe... how about considering letting me be Penny's father..."

Before Julian could finish his sentence, a tall dark figure suddenly lunged out from a shadowed corner.

A furious punch landed squarely on the corner of Julian's mouth!

I had no idea how long Sean Vance had been standing there.

"You bastard! You urged my woman to run away, and now you have the nerve to stand here and try to steal her?!"

"My daughter needs you to be her father? I'd rather be your damn father!"

This was the very first time I had ever heard Sean... swear.

In my memory, a high and mighty heir like him would never utter such rough language.

Sean grabbed Julian by the collar, punching him relentlessly. He held nothing back, every blow drawing blood.

Panicked, I rushed forward to pull him away:

"Stop hitting him! Do you want to kill him?!"

"Sean Vance, stop it right now!"

Sean turned, gripping my hands tightly, his voice a strained, suppressed growl:

"Come with me."

Thinking of Penny upstairs, and remembering her tears and hurt feelings from that night, a blaze of anger surged within me.

"Let go of me!"

"Sean Vance, what right do you have to hit Julian? At least he took care of Penny. What have you ever done as her father?"

"You and Michelle Lu already have a child together! Please stop bothering me!"

"If you love her, then love her wholeheartedly! Penny and I don't need your leftover affection!"

"I don't love her!"

Sean tightened his grip on my wrists, his bloodshot eyes staring fixedly at me.

"I have never loved her!"

"Chloe, listen to me clearly. I have never loved Michelle Lu!"

"The person I, Sean Vance, love... is you, Chloe!"

As he uttered those final words, Sean's breathing grew increasingly ragged, red veins bursting in his eyes.

Like a man running out of oxygen, every single word he spoke caused his chest to contract in agonizing pain.

I stared in shock at his face, which had turned pale without a single drop of color.

Before I could say anything, Sean's hands suddenly went limp.

That tall frame collapsed right in front of my eyes for the first time.

Chapter 6

Sean was rushed straight to the emergency room.

I sat numbly in the waiting area, listening to his personal assistant wipe away tears as he explained:

"Miss Chloe, Mr. Vance has actually been enduring this illness for a very long time..."

"Over four years ago, before you even left, he was already suffering from severe, excruciating pain."

"It was as if simply looking at you or showing warmth toward you would immediately trigger a violent flare-up in his body."

"Back when you escaped... Mr. Vance didn't return to see you for over a month because his body literally couldn't take it anymore."

"Also, a month ago, right after seeing you and Little Miss Penny, he collapsed right outside the bedroom door. Before passing out, he ordered us never to reveal a single word to you."

"For the past month, he has been hospitalized for continuous treatment."

"Today was Little Miss Penny's birthday. He couldn't resist sneaking out of the hospital just to catch a glimpse of her..."

I listened, my mind reeling in absolute shock, rendered completely speechless.

At that exact moment, after five years of silence, the mechanical voice in my head finally chimed again.

It announced:

[3,912th time the male lead has defied orders, going against the flow of the world's plot.]

[The male lead character has endured 1,789 severe electric shocks to his heart as warnings for continuously rebelling against the system to protect the villainous female supporting character.]

[The plot is on the verge of total collapse. The relationship between the male lead, Sean Vance, and the female lead, Michelle Lu, has completely shattered.]

[The male lead character is currently in critical condition near death, triggering a special system emergency protocol.]

[System reset initiating...]

[System reset complete. The male lead's physical condition is entering the recovery phase...]

In that moment when the automated notification sounded, the entire truth unfolded before me.

It turned out that from the very first year I met Sean Vance, he had already fallen for me.

Mainly, he was the male lead, and I was the villainous supporting character. Him loving me meant defying the fundamental rules of this world.

During those years by my side, his body had endured unimaginable pain and torture.

In the first year or two, painkiller injections still had some effect.

Until one instance where he almost lost his life, he finally discovered the existence of the system.

He knew he was the male lead, and he knew I was the supporting character.

He also knew that his destined female lead was not me.

According to the original script, he was supposed to go overseas and spend a passionate night with Michelle Lu.

Yet for my sake, he kept himself clean. The warped storyline caused the person who spent that night with Michelle Lu to become his half-brother instead.

The daughter Michelle Lu gave birth to was actually that brother's flesh and blood.

After his brother died in an accident, he entrusted Sean with taking care of Michelle and the child. Right at that time, the system urged him to marry her as quickly as possible.

Yet Sean adamantly refused.

During that period, he frequently suffered from severe breathing difficulties and constant fainting spells.

Even so, he never entertained the thought of pushing me away, nor did he ever consider letting go.

He simply endured it all in silence, never speaking a single word of it to me.

By the time I got pregnant and made plans to flee, he was already frequently admitted to the hospital.

That trip overseas back then was actually to seek emergency treatment for his recurring heart condition.

Over the past four years, he had turned the world upside down searching for me. No matter how much his body suffered, he never once gave up.

He was gambling.

He gambled that since he was the male lead, if he died, this entire world would collapse along with him.

He loved me, choosing to use his own life as the ultimate bargaining chip.

The system had advised him countless times:

"Male lead, there are plenty of gorgeous women in this world. Just follow the plot, marry Michelle Lu, and you can live happily with other beauties. Why must you insist on choosing Chloe?"

Why indeed?

Why would he do something so foolish?

Sean, you complete idiot.

There was truly no one more foolish than him in this entire world!

I brought Penny to the hospital to stay by Sean's side and care for him.

I also clearly explained to Penny that the little girl who had called on the phone calling him "Daddy" wasn't Sean's child, but his niece.

The little girl was overjoyed to learn that Sean was her real biological father.

The bond of blood between a father and daughter was truly magical. Even though they hadn't grown up together, the two quickly became inseparable and deeply attached to each other.

Half a month after the system reset, he finally woke up.

The attending doctor mentioned that all his test results were completely normal. The severe heart condition that had plagued him was gone without a trace, and his body was now exceptionally healthy.

Standing outside the consultation room hearing those words, tears streamed down my face uncontrollably.

I covered my mouth with my hand, turning around to leave, when I was suddenly wrapped in a firm embrace from behind by a man still wearing hospital clothes.

"Chloe..."

Sean lowered his head, pressing a soft kiss onto the back of my neck, then rested his chin on my shoulder. His voice was low and raspy, yet resolute.

"We won."

"From now on, no one can ever tear us apart again."

My tears fell even faster.

I turned around in his arms, using all my strength to hug him tightly.

"Sean."

"I'm sorry..."

"I misunderstood you."

Sean framed my face with both hands, gently kissing away the tears caught on my lashes.

"It was my fault. I should have explained everything to you clearly instead of hiding it. I made you raise our child all by yourself for nearly five years."

"I have a terrible temper, but I promise I'll change."

"Be good, forgive me, and give me one more chance, okay?"

He suddenly let go of me.

From his pocket, he pulled out a deep red velvet box.

Inside, a rare, priceless gemstone ring sparkled quietly.

"Did you know? I've actually carried this ring with me all the time."

"I made a promise to myself: if I could beat the system, if I won the gamble and gained a healthy body, I would officially propose to you."

"Otherwise, if I could pass away at any moment, I could never bring myself to ruin your life."

"Chloe, will you marry me?"

As soon as his words fell, Sean dropped down on one knee.

I didn't know whether to laugh or cry as I looked at him.

Who on earth proposes on one knee while wearing hospital scrubs?

And what was more... he hadn't even put his shoes on yet!

"Sean, stand up first. The doctor is still inside, and there are so many people gathered around watching..."

I shyly glanced around at the onlookers.

Since when did the top heir of the capital get such thick skin?

Sean looked up at me with intense eyes.

"Be good and nod yes to me. Otherwise..."

I thought he would say he refused to get up.

Unexpectedly, his brows knitted tight, and his voice turned cold with a threat:

"I'll beat that bastard Julian within an inch of his life, then banish him to the far south for the rest of his life, never allowed to return. Do you believe me?"

I let out another helpless laugh.

After letting him slide the ring onto my finger, I leaned down and wrapped my arms around his neck.

"Sigh, there are no fresh flowers, and this is nothing like the dream proposal I had in mind."

"However, Mr. Vance, as long as the person is you, I will nod yes no matter what."

"I love you."

Sean tightened his arms around me, pulling me into a deep, passionate kiss.

"Let's go home. I'll have my mother pick up Penny."

He couldn't help but let out a long sigh:

"After all, if a man's natural weapon is neglected for too long, I'm afraid it might stop working..."

My face instantly flushed red like a ripe cherry.

"Sean Vance!"
