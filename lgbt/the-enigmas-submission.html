I hadn't told a soul about being marked by Mason, far too proud to ever breathe a word of it. "Yeah, got tired of him." Mason had vanished from my life for a full half-month, making the whole ordeal feel like nothing more than a dream. "Good thing you dumped him early. I heard his background isn't simple at all, he..." My mother pushed the door open, cutting my best friend off. She hovered around me in high spirits, beaming at everyone she met. "Mom, why do you look like you're giving away a daughter?" "Why wouldn't I be? The groom is an Enigma! You struck absolute gold, son!" An Enigma?! My entire body went rigid as a terrible premonition instantly washed over me. How could such a coincidence exist in this world? Yet when Mason appeared at the wedding, reality delivered a crushing blow to my face: Not only did it exist, but it was also absurdly coincidental. Mason wore a custom white suit, clearly prepared long in advance. My heart felt as if it stopped beating. Watching him step closer with every stride, my mind was consumed by just two words: "Run away." Before I could even make a move, my feet swung off the ground as Mason swept me up into his arms. A dangerous smile flickered in his eyes. "Sweetheart, I told you not to run. Why don't you ever listen?" I was like prey caught in a net, struggling to no avail. I cast my eyes toward my mother for help, but she merely clapped her hands in excitement, hurriedly ushering us into the bridal suite. My paralyzed mind only began functioning again once the surrounding space turned completely quiet. "Mason..." He lightly bit my lip, swallowing my plea whole. "Call me husband, sweetheart." Now, before everyone's eyes, we had become legal spouses. My mind was a complete jumble right now. "You're the eldest son of the Mu family who was brought back from the orphanage? Why didn't you tell me earlier?!" "If I had told you, you would have run away." Recalling the bar manager's respectful and protective attitude toward Mason that day, I couldn't help but shudder. From the very start, this man had calculated every single move. I thought I had taken in a dog for entertainment, only to be thoroughly played by him. "Wait, why did you willingly become my dog in the first place?" Mason's gaze suddenly filled with immense tenderness. "Because of you." I was stunned, but he gave me no chance to ask further, leaning down for a passionate kiss. "Now, it's time to settle our accounts." Panicking, I backed away. "Mason, how could an Alpha possibly get pregnant?!" He caught my ankle, his hand sliding slowly over my lips, down my throat, and resting on my chest. Finally, his hand settled over my flat lower abdomen, pressing down gently. "You can, right here." I gasped in shock. With the tie cinched tightly around my neck, Mason lowered his head to hide the obsession in his eyes. "It will gradually form." No matter how much I begged, he calmly brushed it off, simply because he had taken off his hearing aid once again. "Sweetheart, do you still dare to run?" "I don't dare anymore, I really don't dare anymore..." Unable to take it any longer, I leaned close to his ear and whispered. As I was pushed to the peak of passion, I blurted out purely on instinct, "Mason, I like you..." Regardless of the dramatic beginning or the calculating motives, this affection had blossomed day by day, rather than being dictated by mere pheromone instincts. Only later did I discover that Mason could actually read lips. He had heard every single plea, including my confession. Mason kept a secret from me. No matter how many times I asked, he refused to reveal why he had willingly acted as my dog. Until one day, I dreamed of two lost children. The younger child sat on the ground, crying his eyes out, while the older child walked over to take his hand. "Be good, don't cry anymore, okay?" "It's all your fault! Buster is dead, he's never coming back!" Buster was the dog that had passed away a few days earlier. The younger child refused to believe it, crying and demanding to look for him. The older child, worried, followed along, only for both of them to get lost. "Buster is gone, but I will stay with you." "In the future, I'll be your dog."
